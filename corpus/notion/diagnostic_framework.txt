Diagnostic Framework v3 (Maya, 2023-09-01)

we keep relearning this stuff. writing it down.

THE FOUR DIMENSIONS

1. Strategy Clarity
   how clear is the path from current state to north star?
   1 = no shared answer to "why are we here"
   2 = leadership aligned, rest of org guessing
   3 = shared narrative, weak link to weekly priorities
   4 = strategy visible in roadmaps and tradeoffs
   5 = a junior IC can articulate the strategy unprompted

2. Operating Velocity
   how fast does the company turn intent into shipped outcomes?
   measured via: avg cycle time, % of bets shipped per quarter, decision latency
   1 = months between decision and visible action
   2 = projects routinely slip past target by >50%
   3 = roadmap mostly tracks, but blockers stack up
   4 = decisions land within days, work flows without escalation
   5 = velocity is a feature; competitors comment on it

3. Team Health
   morale, retention risk, psychological safety, leadership trust
   not a happiness metric. a "will the engine seize next quarter" metric.
   1 = exits are happening or imminent at senior level
   2 = active disengagement, gallows humor
   3 = professional but not energised
   4 = team would defend the company in public
   5 = waitlist of internal hires wanting to join the team

4. Customer Alignment
   how well does the org's daily work track to actual customer outcomes?
   1 = nobody on the team has spoken to a customer in 30 days
   2 = customer feedback exists but doesn't change roadmap
   3 = customer voice is heard, often loses to internal politics
   4 = customer outcomes are reviewed weekly, change priorities
   5 = product team can predict customer feedback before it lands

THE BELOW-3 RULE
if any dimension scores below 3, transformation must not start.
introduced post-Helios-2021. helios scored team health = 2 and we shipped transformation anyway. it failed within 8 weeks. we lost the embed renewal and 60% of the partner relationship.

scoring approach
each partner scores independently after discovery completes
scores are reconciled in a 90-min session, not by averaging
if partners disagree by >1 point, that's a signal worth interrogating, not a number to split

things we've learned the hard way
- don't show clients the framework before scoring. they will lobby.
- don't show clients the scores at all. show the implications.
- the framework is a diagnostic, not a deliverable. don't put it in client decks.
- a 4 in three dimensions and a 1 in one dimension is more dangerous than three 3s. extreme imbalance is a leading indicator of crisis.
