# Discovery Script — Stakeholder Interviews

## Purpose

The Discovery Script standardises stakeholder interviews. Use this script verbatim for the first three interviews of any engagement. Deviate only after interview four, and document the deviation in the engagement log.

## Interview structure (45 minutes)

### Minutes 0–5: Frame
- Confirm recording consent.
- State that nothing they say will be attributed by name in the Problem-Solution-Gap report.
- Ask: "If we have one good conversation today, what would have been worth covering?"

### Minutes 5–20: Problem space
- "Walk me through the last time you felt the company was firing on all cylinders. What was true then?"
- "Walk me through the last time something went badly wrong. What did you see early that others didn't?"
- "What do you think we're going to hear from your colleagues that you're worried might be wrong?"

### Minutes 20–35: Solution attempts
- "What's been tried before to fix this? What worked, what didn't, what got abandoned and why?"
- "If you had a magic wand, what's the one decision the leadership team should make this quarter?"

### Minutes 35–45: Close
- "Who else should we talk to that we haven't put on the list?"
- "What's the question I should have asked but didn't?"

## Anti-patterns

- Do not ask "what's your strategy" directly. People recite the deck. Ask about decisions instead.
- Do not interrupt. The 6 seconds after they stop talking is where the real answer comes out.
- Do not promise outcomes. Discovery is diagnostic, not prescriptive.

## Logistics

Interviews are scheduled in 60-minute slots (45 active + 15 buffer). Recordings are transcribed within 24 hours and stored in the engagement folder. Do not send transcripts to the client — they belong to Northwind.
