# Northwind Advisory — Engagement Lifecycle

## Overview

Every Northwind engagement follows a four-stage lifecycle: Discovery, Diagnostic, Transformation, and Embed. The lifecycle is non-negotiable. Skipping stages is the single most common cause of failed engagements, per the 2023 retrospective.

## Stage 1: Discovery (2 weeks, fixed-fee £25,000)

The Discovery stage establishes the problem space. We interview 6 to 8 stakeholders across executive, operations, and customer-facing roles. Each interview is 45 minutes and follows the Northwind Discovery Script (see `02_discovery_script.md`). We also review the past 12 months of financial reports and customer feedback data.

**Output:** a Problem-Solution-Gap report. This is the only Northwind deliverable that is shared with the entire client leadership team in raw form. No executive summaries, no editing for politics. The discomfort is the point.

## Stage 2: Diagnostic (3 weeks, fixed-fee £40,000)

The Diagnostic scores the client across four dimensions on a 1–5 scale: Strategy Clarity, Operating Velocity, Team Health, and Customer Alignment. A score below 3 in any dimension triggers a deep-dive sub-engagement before the Transformation stage can begin. This rule was introduced in 2021 after the Helios engagement failed because we proceeded to Transformation with a Team Health score of 2.

## Stage 3: Transformation Sprint (£15,000/week, minimum 6 weeks)

Embedded operators work alongside the client team. Northwind partners spend at least 2 days per week onsite during this stage. Remote-only Transformation engagements are not offered — the 2022 review found onsite presence accounted for 60% of the impact.

## Stage 4: Embed (variable, typically 4 weeks)

The Embed stage transfers knowledge and tooling to the client's permanent team. Without a successful Embed, the work erodes within 90 days. Embed is the most underbilled stage and historically the one clients try to cut. We refuse engagements that exclude Embed.

## Refunds

Clients may request a full refund within 14 days of kickoff. After kickoff, fees are non-refundable but credit may be applied to future engagements.
